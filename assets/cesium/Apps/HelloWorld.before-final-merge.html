Permits & Restricted Areas
Indians need no permit to enter Sikkim. Foreigners need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) just to enter, plus a permit below for restricted areas. Rules can change — verify locally before travel.
Nathula Pass
Indians: Permit required (fee: verify with official source). Foreigners: NOT allowed, no exceptions.
Tsomgo (Changu) Lake / Baba Mandir
Indians and foreigners: Permit required.
Zuluk / Old Silk Route
Indians: Permit required. Foreigners: NOT allowed.
North Sikkim: Lachen, Lachung, Yumthang, Zero Point
Indians and foreigners: Permit required, usually via registered tour operator.
Gurudongmar Lake
Indians: Permit required. Foreigners: NOT allowed.
Dzongri / Goechala Trek
Indians and foreigners: Trekking permit required.
